App icon

The two hemispheres with one line crossing the join, ending at a station: you, arriving. It works without the name, so it holds up whatever we call the app.

Name

Hemisphere has been the working name. Here are the strongest options, all set in the app's wordmark style.

My pick
northbound

Cape Town to the UK is due north, and "northbound" is what a platform sign says. It joins the transit map and the journey in one word.

hemisphere

Says the split-screen idea exactly. Slightly abstract as a name for a planning tool.

halfway

Friendly and short, and the number sits halfway between the two places. Could sound unfinished.

farside

The other side of the world. Distinctive, but leans more poetic than practical.
