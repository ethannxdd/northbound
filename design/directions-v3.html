Hemisphere and Interchange, as one app

Hemisphere becomes the home screen and the overall look. Interchange becomes the plan: your move as a transit map. Money lines are Cape sun yellow because the money comes from Cape Town, paperwork is mill brick, and the move itself is Atlantic blue.

Dark mode is night in both places: Cape Town turns to dusk, Manchester to a deep Atlantic blue, and the station sign lights up like a real one after dark. The numbers are placeholders.
